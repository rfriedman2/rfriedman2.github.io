What Changed in v4

// Summary of Merges from Their Plan
  • Tempo added to every exercise — 3-sec negatives, 1–2 sec holds
  • Pyramid loading on all compound movements
  • Shoulder warm-up circuit on Day 1 and Day 5
  • Drop set added to leg press final set
  • Leg extension becomes a 5-set opener on leg day (their technique)
  • Seated leg curl replaces lying curl — their variation, growing weight
  • Adductor 10-sec isometric hold adopted
  • Cable pullover opener added to back day
  • Hammer Strength high row added to back day
  • Preacher curl added to arms day
  • Hanging leg lifts and reverse crunches added to abs
  • EZ-bar lying extension replaces skull crushers (same movement, safer)

What Was Kept Out

// Their Exercises Not Adopted & Why
  • Front squats with barbell — spinal compression risk at 60+; leg press covers the stimulus
  • Skull crushers — replaced with EZ-bar lying extension; identical movement, safer wrist and elbow angle
  • Heavy flat barbell bench — machine press substituted; same stimulus, fixed path protects rotator cuff
  • Smith machine overhead press — seated DB press preferred; natural arc of motion, less impingement risk
  • Lying cable curls with partials — not adopted; high elbow fatigue on top of an already full bicep day
  • Bicep machine (their ex. 8) — hammer curl and reverse curl cover the same ground more efficiently

What Makes This Plan Different

// Key upgrades from the merge
  • Tempo on every exercise — 3-sec negatives, 1–2 sec holds at peak
  • Pyramid loading on all compound movements — grow weight each set
  • Shoulder warm-up circuit on chest and shoulder days
  • Drop set added to leg press final set
  • Leg extension becomes 5-set pyramid opener on leg day
  • Seated leg curl replaces lying curl — growing weight each set
  • Adductor 10-sec isometric hold after every set
  • Cable pullover opener added to back day
  • Hammer Strength high row added to back day
  • Preacher curl added to arms day
  • Hanging leg lifts and reverse crunches added to abs
  • EZ-bar lying extension replaces skull crushers — same stimulus, safer joints

Recovery Rules

// Non-negotiable for 60+
  • Sleep 7–9 hours — growth hormone peaks during deep sleep
  • Tue & Fri are rest days — never train 3 days back-to-back
  • Arms follow Legs by design — arms arrive fully fresh every session
  • Deload every 6–8 weeks: cut weight 40%, keep reps — CNS reset
  • 10 min mobility work daily, even on rest days
  • Pain vs. soreness: DOMS is fine, joint pain is a stop sign
  • Annual bloodwork: testosterone, thyroid, CBC, lipids, Vitamin D
  • Rest day activity: 20–30 min easy walking only
